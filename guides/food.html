Eating Well on a Student Budget

You first year s busy: new classes, new friends, and long days on campus. It is easy to skip meals or spend your whole allowance or milk tea. Eating regular, simple meals helps you focus in class, and you need a big budget to do it.

Where to Eat Near Campus

You have more options than you think. Here are the usual go-to spots:

  • The school canteen for quick rice meals between classes
  • Carinderias outside the gate for cheap, filling ulam and rice
  • Your own baon from home, which is the cheapest choice of all
  • Convenience store for emergency snacks, not for every meal

How to Plan your Weekly Food Budget

A little planning on sunday saves you from being broke by Thursday.

  1. Check how much allowance you have for the whole week.
  2. Set aside money for fare and school needs first.
  3. Divide what is left by the number of school days.
  4. Pack baon at least two days to stretch your budget.
  5. Keep a small amount for energencies, like a surprise project.

Quick Tips

Always bring a refillable water bottle Water stations on campus are free, and you will save a lot compared to buying bottled drinks every day.
